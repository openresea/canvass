How to record a visit
  1. Choose the street, house and unit you’re visiting. Add missing details using the links below each field.
  2. Select a resident, or choose “Name not known” or “Whole household”.
  3. Record what happened. Only ask the survey questions if the resident wants to take part.
  4. Save the visit. The address stays selected so you can choose the next resident.
